Points 50 + 50 with video Build on ai.pharos.xyz What you'll build A monitor that watches a Pharos contract for a specific event (e.g. `Transfer`, `Deposit`) and reports each new occurrence as it happens. It should keep running, not take a one-off snapshot. Requirements 01 Uses Chain MCP log queries filtered by contract address and event signature. 02 Runs continuously (polling or block-by-block) and reports only new events, with no duplicates. 03 Decodes each event into readable fields (from, to, amount, tx hash, block). 04 Demonstrated catching at least one live or test event. Reviewers score against this list. Missing items can mean partial points. What to submit A proof link reviewers can open — a share link from ai.pharos.xyz, a tx hash on the explorer, or a deployed demo. A repo link if you wrote code.

GAS FEE OPTIMIZER

ready-for-review

Arena: in-review

Deadline dispatch now releases disk locks before network/receipt waits. Actual wall-clock demo broadcasts both jobs before their deadlines while mining is paused and a quote is slow; receipts report actual fees, restart sends zero duplicates.

Local EVM chain31337 with controlled quotes, 200ms lead, 20ms polling and a responsive disposable signer/RPC. Deadline means node acceptance, not mining. Outages or host suspension remain observable failures; no unconditional real-world timing guarantee.

Arena requirements · Source and run instructions

  • gas-worker.json
  • gas.json
Organizer checklist
01
Tracks gas price over time and shows recent history.
02
Accepts a transaction with a deadline and holds it until gas is favorable or the deadline is reached.
03
Reports the actual gas paid compared with the price at request time.
04
Never misses the deadline. It submits at the deadline even if gas is high.

SAGA EXECUTOR

ready-for-review

Arena: in-review

Three dependent steps verified by local EVM receipts, bounded safe retries and compensation after a real mined revert.

Lab accounting is reversible 1:1. Real swaps cannot roll back atomically and unwind can lose value. Unknown broadcast/receipt states require reconciliation; never blindly rebroadcast.

Arena requirements · Source and run instructions

  • saga-success.json
  • saga-forced-failure.json
Organizer checklist
01
At least 3 dependent steps, each verified (receipt checked) before the next starts.
02
A defined retry policy for transient failures, e.g. a gas spike or dropped transaction.
03
A defined compensation/rollback path when a step permanently fails, and a demo of it.
04
A state log showing exactly which step the saga is in at any time.
05
Shows one fully successful run and one forced-failure run with recovery.

Reads two local EVM reference-rate contracts with controlled prices; simulated execution explicitly allowed. Profit is a quoted estimate, not an executable market guarantee.

YIELD OPTIMIZER

ready-for-review

Arena: in-review

Three fresh Pharos vault yields compared at block20186816. Cost-policy wrapper reports conditional net gain, break-even gas, minimum holding, high-cost stay-put and unknown-cost rejection; never guesses a PROS exchange rate.

Fee allowances and USDC gas budget are explicitly configured scenario inputs, not independently verified investor costs. Recommendation is conditional on those inputs and historical returns repeating. No executable route, eligibility or future yield guarantee.

Arena requirements · Source and run instructions

  • yield-policy.json
  • yield-policy-mcp.jsonl
Organizer checklist
01
Compares at least 3 yield sources using live on-chain data.
02
Accounts for gas, entry/exit fees, and a minimum holding period before recommending a move.
03
Outputs a clear recommendation with expected net gain, or "stay put" with the reason.
04
If executing: prepares transactions for user signature only. The agent never holds keys.

ready-for-review

Arena: in-review

Two funded local vaults share one query/action interface: highest-yield deposit, lowest-yield withdrawal, successful unsigned preflights, receipt-verified lab execution, outage isolation and insufficient-allowance rejection.

Public Centrifuge eligibility/KYC and asynchronous settlement remain distinct; inspected mainnet addresses were blocked. No demo video. Organizer acceptance of local demonstrations is unconfirmed.

Arena requirements · Source and run instructions

  • live-result.json
Organizer checklist
01
Integrates at least 2 protocols or vaults behind one interface.
02
Supports both queries (positions, rates) and actions (deposit or withdraw prepared for signature).

04
